Chapter 1 · Rule 3

Protect your sleep like an important appointment

Why it matters

Sleep is when your body repairs tissue, consolidates memory and regulates mood and hormones. Men often treat sleeplessness as a badge of honour. The evidence says it is a slow leak in your health, your temper and your judgment.

What the evidence says

General guidance for adults is 7 to 9 hours a night, and poor sleep tends to worsen both mental and physical health problems [28]. A large US study reported by the World Economic Forum found that men with all five healthy sleep habits, which include sleeping seven to eight hours and not depending on sleep medication, had a life expectancy 4.7 years longer than men with none or only one, and were 30 percent less likely to die prematurely [3].

Do this: Fix a wake-up time and keep it, even on weekends. Cut caffeine in the afternoon, keep the bedroom dark and cool, and put the phone outside the bedroom.
